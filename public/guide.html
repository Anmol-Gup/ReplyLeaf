Your WhatsApp number

Replyleaf connects through WhatsApp's official Business platform. On the Connect page you press Connect WhatsApp and complete the steps in Meta's window. Which number you can use depends on where it lives today.

A new, separate number (best)

A fresh SIM that is not on WhatsApp yet. Use it only for the business and do not share it with friends or family, so only customers write to it. Once it is connected through Replyleaf, you answer from the dashboard.

Trade-off: you read and reply in the Replyleaf dashboard, not in a phone app.

WhatsApp Business app

Your number is already in the WhatsApp Business app. You can connect it and keep using the app on your phone at the same time. Needs a recent app version (2.24.17 or newer).

Note: messages from your friends and family on this number will also reach Replyleaf, and the assistant may reply to them. Reply to those chats yourself from your phone and the assistant steps aside.

Personal WhatsApp

A number used in regular personal WhatsApp can't be connected as it is. Install the WhatsApp Business app on that number first (your chats move across), then connect it. Follow Meta's own prompts in the window.

If you keep the WhatsApp Business app (coexistence)

  • When you reply yourself from the phone, the assistant steps aside for that chat. It shows as "waiting for human" in Conversations.
  • To hand the chat back to the assistant, press Resume assistant in the dashboard.
  • Open the WhatsApp Business app on your phone every week or two. WhatsApp can disconnect the link if the app stays unused for a long time. Details: WhatsApp's guide for Business app users.
  • Group chats are not connected. Only one-to-one customer chats are.
Friends and family: the assistant answers anyone who messages the connected number. If you use the number personally, reply from your phone to those chats yourself, so the assistant stays out of them.

WhatsApp free allowance and payment

WhatsApp (Meta) charges for messages, not Replyleaf. When a customer writes to you, you can reply for the next 24 hours. That is the only time the assistant can send normal messages.

  • Meta gives some free messages per phone number each month and charges per message after that. Meta sets the rules and has changed them before, so the exact free amount for your number can differ.
  • Replyleaf's Usage page counts your delivered messages and shows an estimate. It also detects from Meta's own delivery reports whether your number gets the free allowance.
  • Read Meta's current prices here: Pricing on the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • If the customer has been silent for more than 24 hours, a normal reply is not sent. The dashboard marks it "not sent" and shows a warning.

Add a payment method for WhatsApp charges

Meta may ask for a payment method on your WhatsApp Business account. Charges beyond the free allowance go to it. Menu names can differ a little by country and account.

  1. Open Meta Business Settings (business.facebook.com) and sign in with the account that owns your WhatsApp Business account.
  2. Go to Accounts → WhatsApp accounts and select your WhatsApp Business account.
  3. Open Payment settings and click Add payment method. Enter your card details and save.
  4. You can also use Billing & payments → Payment methods, then attach the method to your WhatsApp account in step 3. A card saved only at business level is not enough.
  5. If Meta asks for business tax details, add them in your business settings, then try again.
  6. Open WhatsApp Manager and check that the "missing payment method" warning is gone.

If your screen looks different, search "add payment method WhatsApp" in the Meta Business Help Center.

The AI part

Your own AI key

  1. Create a key with Google (Gemini) or OpenAI, and add billing there if the provider asks for it.
  2. In Replyleaf go to Settings → AI, pick the provider and paste the key.
  3. Replyleaf checks it with the provider, then stores it encrypted. Only the last four characters are ever shown again.
  • If the key is missing or refused, the assistant switches off. Customers get a short "our team will reply" message and the chat moves to Human handoff, so nothing is lost. You are emailed once a day. Saving a working key turns it back on.
  • If the AI provider is briefly down, the customer gets one friendly "temporary delay" message and the reply follows when it recovers. After about 10 minutes the chat goes to Human handoff.

Teach it your business

  • Knowledge base → core knowledge is always read. Put your services, prices, address, hours and contact email and phone there. The assistant only knows what is written there and never makes up a number or an email.
  • Longer material (brochures, FAQs) goes in as documents. The assistant picks the relevant parts for each question.
  • Update the knowledge whenever prices or hours change.

Business hours

Set your working days, time zone and hours in Settings → Workflows → Business hours. Appointments have their own hours in the Appointment requests card. After hours, the assistant still answers normal questions. Only when someone asks for a person does it say you are away, when you are back, and share your contact details from the knowledge base.

Appointments, tickets and leads

The assistant can collect three kinds of requests from WhatsApp. Switch each one on or off in Settings → Workflows. Replyleaf only saves what the customer has confirmed, and you handle everything from the dashboard.

  • It asks one or two questions at a time, shows a short summary, and saves the request only after the customer says yes.
  • You get an email for each new request. Choose who gets it in Settings → Notifications.
  • It may ask for an email address for updates. The customer can skip it and nothing is held back.
  • The Overview page shows what needs you: requests to confirm, open tickets, new leads and follow-ups due.

Appointments

The assistant collects the purpose, preferred date and time, and the customer's name. It saves a pending request. It never says a slot is free or confirmed, because there is no calendar connection.

  • You then talk to the customer yourself (the Appointments page has a WhatsApp link).
  • Set the status: Confirmed, Rescheduled, Completed, No show or Cancelled. Confirmed and Rescheduled need the agreed date and time.
  • Tick Notify customer to tell them: by WhatsApp if their 24-hour window is open, otherwise by email, otherwise nothing is sent and you are told.
  • Have a Calendly or booking page? Put the link in the Appointment requests card and the assistant shares it instead.

Tickets (problems)

When a customer reports a problem, the assistant asks what went wrong, an order or booking reference, and what they would like. It logs a ticket with a reference like T-XXXXXX.

  • It never promises a refund, a replacement or a time to fix it.
  • Priority is High only when the customer says it is urgent.
  • You set the status (Open, In progress, Waiting for customer, Resolved, Closed), assign a teammate by email, and keep private internal notes.
  • Optionally tell the customer when the status changes.
  • Set your own categories and an expected response time in Settings.

Leads (project enquiries)

For new business enquiries the assistant asks the questions you set (your services and your own custom questions in Settings), confirms a summary and saves a lead as New.

  • Only you move a lead on: Qualified, Contacted, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won or Lost.
  • Add a follow-up date, details and private notes.
  • Copy or download the project brief to share with your team. It leaves out your internal notes.
  • The same enquiry sent twice is saved once.
If a request cannot be saved for any reason, the customer is told your team will follow up and the chat goes to Human handoff, so nothing is lost.

Day to day

  • Conversations: see every chat. Use Take over to reply yourself. The assistant stays silent in that chat until you hand it back.
  • Human handoff: chats that need a person. Reply within the 24-hour window.
  • Usage: replies, tokens and WhatsApp messages this month.
  • Contacts: edit a customer's details, or delete a person's data on request.
