# Bright Smile Dental Clinic (fictional sample business)

## About
Bright Smile Dental Clinic is a family dental practice at 12 Park Street, Springfield. Phone: 555-0142. We speak English and Spanish (hablamos español). The clinic is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible. Free parking is available behind the building.

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday: 9:00 to 18:00
Saturday: 9:00 to 13:00
Sunday: closed

## Services and prices
- Check-up and cleaning: $60 (about 45 minutes)
- Children's check-up (under 12): $40
- Teeth whitening: $180
- Cavity filling: from $90 per tooth
- Root canal: from $450
- Braces consultation: free
- Emergency visit: $120 call-out fee, same day when a slot is free

## Policies
- Cancellations: please give at least 24 hours notice. Late cancellations or no-shows are charged a $25 fee.
- Payment: cash or card. We do not bill insurance companies directly, but we give you an itemised receipt you can submit to your insurer.
- First visit: arrive 10 minutes early and bring photo ID and any previous x-rays.
